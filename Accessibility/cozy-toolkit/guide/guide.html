✨ Getting Started

Click the Cozy Accessibility icon in your browser toolbar to open the popup. Use the Enable Cozy toggle to turn all features on or off.

One-Click Presets

Not sure where to start? Try a preset — each one activates a curated set of features:

  • 📖 Dyslexia — OpenDyslexic font, reading ruler, warm tint overlay
  • ⚡ ADHD — Paragraph focus, distraction hiding, bionic reading, sensory controls
  • 🧘 Sensory — Freeze animations, calm overlay, minimal distractions
  • 👁 Low Vision — Large Atkinson Hyperlegible font, high contrast, large cursor

You can always fine-tune individual settings after applying a preset.

📖 Reading Tab

🌈 Color Tint

Adds a colored overlay to reduce visual stress. Adjust the tint color, opacity, brightness, contrast, and color temperature. Great for reducing harsh white backgrounds.

Color Filter

Simulates color vision filters for protanopia, deuteranopia, or tritanopia — useful for testing or adjusting page colors for your needs.

💡 Bionic Reading

Bolds the first part of each word to create visual anchors that guide your eyes. Choose from Light, Medium, or Strong intensity.

📖 Reading Mode

Strips away page clutter and shows just the main article content. Adjust the max width and background color for a clean reading experience.

🔤 Typography Tab

🔤 Reading Font

Replace page fonts with a more readable option:

  • OpenDyslexic — Weighted bottoms to prevent letter flipping
  • Lexend — Designed to reduce visual stress and improve reading fluency
  • Atkinson Hyperlegible — Maximizes letter differentiation for low vision
  • System Default — Uses your OS default font

Fine-tune font size, line height, letter spacing, word spacing, and weight to find your perfect combo.

✍️ Writing Comfort

Applies your chosen dyslexia-friendly font and spacing to text inputs and textareas — so forms and editors feel just as cozy as the rest of the page.

🧘 Sensory Tab

Calm down overwhelming pages with these controls:

  • Freeze animations — Stops CSS animations and transitions
  • Hide GIFs — Replaces animated GIFs with a static placeholder
  • Reduce motion — Applies prefers-reduced-motion styles across the page
  • Pause autoplay — Stops videos from playing automatically
  • Large cursor — Makes your cursor bigger and easier to track

🎯 Focus Tab

🔍 Paragraph Focus

Dims everything except the paragraph you're reading. Use j/k keys to move between paragraphs, or click to lock on one. Adjust the dim level to control how much the surrounding content fades.

📏 Reading Ruler

A horizontal guide strip that follows your cursor. Adjust its height, opacity, and color to match your preference.

🛡️ Hide Distractions

Removes common page clutter:

  • Sidebars — Side navigation and recommendation panels
  • Ads — Common ad containers
  • Cookie banners — Consent popups and GDPR notices
  • Floating elements — Sticky headers, chat widgets, notification bars
  • Comments — Comment sections below articles
  • Social/share — Social media buttons and share widgets

🔊 Text-to-Speech Tab

Enable TTS, then select any text on a page to hear it read aloud. Adjust the reading speed and choose from your system's available voices.

Works with any language your browser supports.

⚙️ Site Settings Tab

Per-Site Disable

Turn off Cozy for specific websites. The current site's domain is shown — click Disable here to exempt it. Click again to re-enable.

Reset to Defaults

Resets all settings back to their original values. Your disabled sites list is preserved.

Settings Page

Click the gear icon (⚙️) in the popup header to open the full Settings page, where you can:

  • Export your settings as a JSON backup file
  • Import settings from a backup
  • Manage your disabled sites list
  • Reset everything to factory defaults

🎨 Themes

Cozy Accessibility comes with 4 themes — pick one from the dropdown in the top-right corner:

  • Retro Pink — The classic LoveSpark look. Warm pink gradients and glass surfaces.
  • Dark — Deep purple-black with pink neon accents and starfield background.
  • Beige — Warm, paper-like tones with serif typography. Calm and bookish.
  • Slate — Minimal dark gray with orange accents. Clean and modern.

Your theme choice syncs across the popup, settings page, and this guide.

🔒 Privacy

Cozy Accessibility collects zero data. All your settings are stored locally in your browser. No analytics, no tracking, no remote requests. Fonts are bundled — nothing is loaded from the internet. Free and open-source, always.
