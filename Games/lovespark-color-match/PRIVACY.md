# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Color Match

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Color Match is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

Your game statistics (games won today, total games won, your best time, and the date used to reset the daily count), an on/off setting for the toolbar badge, and your chosen theme. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Color Match makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. Fonts are bundled inside the extension, not loaded from a font service. No data ever leaves your device.

The popup footer contains two ordinary links (the LoveSpark website, lovespark.love, and a Ko-fi tip page). They open in a new tab only if you click them, and the extension sends no data with them.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your game stats (games won, best time), badge setting and theme locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
