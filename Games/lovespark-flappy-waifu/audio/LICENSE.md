## Track shortlist (audited 2026-05-05)

Three CC0 candidates were evaluated against the criteria in `~/.claude/plans/let-us-update-flappy-snazzy-quasar.md`:

1. **Chill lofi inspired** — omfgdude — https://opengameart.org/content/chill-lofi-inspired — 2.7 MB — jazzy lofi hip hop, piano + drums. **Selected.**
2. Calm Ambient 1 (Synthwave 4k) — The Cynic Project — https://opengameart.org/content/calm-ambient-1-synthwave-4k — 6.3 MB — relaxing pads, soothing chords. Rejected: pad-heavy frequencies overlap the 380–520 Hz flap SFX band.
3. Calm Ambient 2 (Synthwave 15k) — The Cynic Project — https://opengameart.org/content/calm-ambient-2-synthwave-15k — 8.3 MB — relaxing ambient pads + decorative elements. Rejected: same frequency masking concern, plus 3× the bundle weight.

**Selection rationale**: smallest bundle weight, cleanest frequency separation (piano keys mostly above 1 kHz, drum kick/snare punchy in the 60–200 Hz band), and lofi-hiphop reads as "deliberate taste" on the kawaii-arcade portfolio without being aggressive enough to repeat the prior "annoying whistling" failure mode.
