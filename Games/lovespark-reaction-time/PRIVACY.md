# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Reaction Time

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Reaction Time is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

Your reaction-test results and preferences: attempts today and in total, your personal best time, the last 50 reaction times from today (used for the daily average), the date of the last daily reset, whether the extension is enabled, and your chosen theme. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Reaction Time makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. Fonts are bundled with the extension. No data ever leaves your device. The popup footer contains two ordinary links (lovespark.love and ko-fi.com) that open in a new tab only if you click them.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your attempt counts, personal best, today's times, enabled state and theme locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
