# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Word Scramble

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Word Scramble is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

Your game stats (words solved today, total words solved, current and best streak, and the date used for the daily reset), whether the toolbar badge counter is enabled, and your chosen theme. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Word Scramble makes no network requests of its own. Words, fonts and images are all packaged inside the extension. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. No data ever leaves your device.

The popup footer contains two ordinary links (lovespark.love and a Ko-fi page). They only open in a new tab if you click them, and no data from the extension is sent with them.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your game stats, streaks, badge on/off setting and theme locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
