# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Zen Garden

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Zen Garden is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

Your garden layout (the rocks and plants you place, up to 30 items), a count of garden sessions today and in total, the date of the last daily reset, an on/off flag, and your theme choice. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. The clear-garden button erases your garden layout; uninstalling the extension removes all of this data.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Zen Garden makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. Fonts are bundled with the extension, not loaded from the web. No data ever leaves your device.

The popup footer has links to lovespark.love and ko-fi.com. They open in a new tab only if you click them.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your garden layout, session counts, on/off setting and theme locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
