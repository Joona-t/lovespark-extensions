# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Quick Save

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

> This extension is archived and no longer maintained.

LoveSpark Quick Save is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

When you click "Save This Page", the address (URL) and title of the current tab, plus the time you saved it, are added to your saved-links list (up to 50 entries). The extension also stores a today/total save counter, the date of the last daily counter reset, an on/off flag, and your theme preference. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. You can delete individual saved links from the popup, or remove everything by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Quick Save makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. No data ever leaves your device. Saved links open in a new tab only when you click them.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your list of saved links, save counters, on/off state and theme locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit. The popup reads only the address and title of the active tab, and only at the moment you click "Save This Page".

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
