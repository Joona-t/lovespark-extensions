CWS Filler

Change Theme

Auto-fills the Chrome Web Store dashboard from a cws-listing.json.

  1. Open your extension's listing in the CWS dashboard.
  2. Click the Sparky button at the bottom-right of the page.
  3. Load its cws-listing.json, then Fill text fields.
  4. Drag in the screenshots & tiles, review, and click Submit yourself.

Text fields only — images & category stay manual. Nothing leaves your device.
