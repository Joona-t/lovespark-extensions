# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Calm Down Kit

**Effective date:** 2026-06-09 · **Version:** 1.0.0

LoveSpark Calm Down Kit is a free, open-source browser extension that helps you manage ADHD emotional dysregulation: you rate your current intensity and the extension matches you to a DBT-inspired, ADHD-adapted calming technique. Your privacy is built into the design — everything happens locally, on your own device.

**The short version:** We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit any data. Nothing leaves your browser. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no ads.

---

## What we store

The extension keeps a small amount of data **on your own device only**, using the browser's built-in `chrome.storage.local`. This never leaves your computer. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

- **Your preferences and settings** — for example your selected theme, accessibility options, and any technique categories you've turned on or off.
- **Your most recent or matched technique** — so the kit can show you the right calming technique for the intensity you rated.
- **Local usage data** — such as counters, streaks, or a count of calming sessions completed, used only to display your own progress back to you.
- **Optional in-the-moment notes** — if a feature lets you jot a note or save a favorite technique, that text is stored locally with the rest of your settings.

All of this lives only in your browser's local storage. You can erase it at any time by removing the extension or clearing the extension's storage from your browser settings. Uninstalling the extension deletes this data.

We do **not** store your name, email, location, browsing history, the contents of pages you visit, or any other personally identifying information.

---

## What we transmit

**Zero data is transmitted externally. Ever.**

- No data is sent to LoveSpark, the developer, or any third party.
- There are **no servers** — the extension has nothing to phone home to.
- There is **no analytics**, **no telemetry**, **no tracking**, and **no advertising**.
- No remote code is downloaded or executed. The extension runs entirely from the code shipped in the package you installed.

Everything the extension does — rating intensity, matching a technique, remembering your settings — happens on your device, offline.

---

## Permissions

This extension declares exactly **one** permission, and only because the feature below requires it:

- **`storage`** — Used solely to save your settings, preferences, and local progress (such as counters or streaks) on your own device via `chrome.storage.local`, so the kit remembers your choices between sessions. This permission does **not** grant any network access and is never used to transmit data anywhere.

We request no other permissions. The extension follows the principle of least privilege: if a permission isn't needed for a feature you can see, we don't ask for it.

---

## Host permissions

**None.**

This extension requests **no host permissions** and **no access to any website**. It cannot read, modify, or monitor the pages you visit, and it does not run any code on the websites you browse.

Because there are no host permissions, the extension specifically does **not**:

- collect or read your browsing history,
- read the content of any web page,
- inject ads, trackers, or any scripts into the sites you visit,
- monitor your activity across the web.

---

## Third-party services

**None.**

The extension uses no third-party services, SDKs, APIs, fonts loaded from external servers, analytics providers, advertising networks, or cloud backends. There are no external dependencies that receive your data, because no data is ever sent anywhere.

---

## Children's privacy

This extension collects **no personal data from anyone**, including children under the age of 13. Because nothing is collected, stored remotely, or transmitted, there is no personal information that could be exposed. The Calm Down Kit is safe for users of all ages.
