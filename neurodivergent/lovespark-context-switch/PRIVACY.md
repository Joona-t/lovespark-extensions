# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Context Switch

**Effective date:** 2026-07-08 · **Applies to:** LoveSpark Context Switch v1.0.29

LoveSpark Context Switch helps you notice how often you switch tasks. It logs the context switches you record, counts them per day, and surfaces your patterns over time — all on your own device. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the extension does and does not do with your data.

**The short version:** This extension stores everything locally in your browser and transmits nothing. No account, no servers, no analytics, no tracking. Always free.

---

## What we store

Everything the extension keeps lives in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your own computer. Depending on how you use it, this includes:

- **Your logged context switches** — the entries you create when you record a task switch (for example, a timestamp and any note or label you attach to it).
- **Daily counters** — the number of context switches counted for each day, used to show your per-day totals.
- **Streaks and derived stats** — any streaks, running totals, or summary figures computed from your own logged entries so the extension can show you your patterns.
- **Your settings and preferences** — options such as your chosen theme and any display or behavior preferences you set.

That data stays on your device. Uninstalling the extension, or clearing its storage through your browser, removes it.

---

## What we transmit

**Zero.** No data is transmitted externally, ever.

- No data is sent to us or to any third party.
- There are no servers, no backend, and no cloud sync.
- There is no analytics, no telemetry, no usage tracking, no crash reporting, and no advertising.
- Your logged switches, counters, streaks, and settings never leave your browser.

---

## Permissions

The extension declares exactly one permission, and it is used only for the extension's stated purpose:

- **`storage`** — Used to save your context-switch log, your daily counters and streaks, and your settings locally in your browser so they persist between sessions and browser restarts. This permission does not grant, and is not used for, any network access or data transmission.

The extension requests no other permissions.

---

## Host permissions

**None.** The extension requests no host permissions and no access to any website.

Because it has no host access, it cannot and does not:

- read, monitor, or collect your browsing history or the pages you visit,
- read or modify the content of any website you open,
- inject ads, trackers, scripts, or any code into web pages.

---

## Third-party services

**None.** The extension bundles no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, advertising networks, or external services. It makes no calls to any outside service and loads no remote code. All logic runs locally from the code shipped with the extension.

---

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because all data stays on the user's own device and nothing is transmitted, the extension is safe for users of all ages.

---

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the extension's public source repository, and the effective date and version above will be updated. Because the repository is public, the full history of changes is visible to anyone.
