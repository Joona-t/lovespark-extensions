# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Desk Stretch

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Desk Stretch is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

Your stretch counters (stretches done today and in total, plus the date of the last daily reset), whether the extension is switched on, which stretch was shown last (so the next one is different), and your theme preference. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Desk Stretch makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. The stretch list is built into the extension. No data ever leaves your device.

The popup footer contains two ordinary links (lovespark.love and ko-fi.com/joonat). They open in a new tab only if you click them; nothing is sent by the extension.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your stretch counters, on/off setting, last-shown stretch and theme locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
