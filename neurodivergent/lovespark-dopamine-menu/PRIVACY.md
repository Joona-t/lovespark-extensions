# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Dopamine Menu

**Effective date:** 2026-07-08 · **Applies to version:** 1.0.29 and later

LoveSpark Dopamine Menu is a free, open-source browser extension built with privacy as a first principle. It runs entirely on your own device. It does **not** collect personal information, it does **not** send anything to any server, and there are **no accounts, no ads, and no trackers** — ever.

## What we store

The extension keeps a small amount of data on your own device using the browser's built-in `chrome.storage.local` store. This data never leaves your computer. It includes:

- **Your settings and preferences** — such as your chosen theme and any display options.
- **Completion counters and streaks** — the number of dopamine-menu items you've tapped as "done," and any per-day or streak totals used to show your progress.
- **Which curated items you've marked done** — the state that powers the "tap to mark done, track completions" feature.

That's it. There is no profile, no identity, and nothing that identifies you personally. If you uninstall the extension or clear your browser's extension data, this information is gone with it.

## What we transmit

**Zero data is transmitted externally.** The extension does not contain any analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or "phone-home" code. It makes no network requests to LoveSpark or to any third party, and there is no LoveSpark server that receives your information. Everything the extension does happens locally, inside your browser.

## Permissions

The extension requests only the minimum permissions needed for its single purpose. Each one is explained below.

- **`storage`** — Used to save your settings, completion counters, streaks, and which items you've marked done, all in `chrome.storage.local` on your device. This is what lets your progress persist between sessions. It is **not** used to transmit, sync, or share any data off your device.

## Host permissions

**None.** The extension requests no host permissions and no access to any website. It cannot read, monitor, or modify the pages you visit; it has no ability to see your browsing history; and it never injects ads, trackers, or any other content into web pages. Its interface is a self-contained popup with a curated list of healthy quick activities — nothing more.

## Third-party services

**None.** The extension uses no third-party SDKs, no external APIs, no fonts or scripts loaded from remote servers, and no analytics providers. Nothing about your use of the extension is shared with any outside party, because there are no outside parties involved.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects **no personal data from anyone**, including children under 13. Because nothing is collected, stored off-device, or transmitted, there is nothing that could put a child's privacy at risk. It is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published in the extension's public repository, and the **Effective date** and **Applies to version** line above will be updated. Because the project is open source, the full history of any changes is publicly visible in the repository's commit history.
