# LoveSpark Dopamine Menu

Dopamine menu for ADHD & neurodivergent focus — pick a quick healthy stim, tap done, track your completions. Free, no account.

Part of the [LoveSpark Suite](https://github.com/Joona-t) — retro pink productivity tools for a calmer internet.

## Why

Feeling restless, understimulated, or stuck in a scroll spiral? Instead of reaching for the endless feed, glance at a curated menu of small, doable options — a stretch, a glass of water, a short walk, a song, a few deep breaths — choose one, and tap it done.

Built for ADHD and neurodivergent brains that crave stimulation but get overwhelmed by open-ended "just do something productive" advice. A short, concrete menu removes the decision paralysis: you're not inventing an activity, you're picking one off a list.

## Features

- Opens a curated menu of quick, healthy dopamine "stims" in one click
- Tap any item to mark it done and log the completion
- Tracks your completion count so progress is visible
- Neurodivergent-first design: OpenDyslexic font, retro-pink theme, high-contrast, reduced-motion friendly
- Fully keyboard-navigable with screen-reader labels
- Works entirely offline — everything stays on your device
- No streak-shaming, no flashing, no guilt
