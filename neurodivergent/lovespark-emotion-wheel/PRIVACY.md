# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Emotion Wheel

**Effective date:** 2026-06-09 · **Applies to:** version 1.0.1

LoveSpark Emotion Wheel is a free, open-source browser extension that helps you identify feelings — from basic to nuanced — using an interactive emotion wheel. It is built privacy-first: everything happens on your own device, and nothing you do is ever sent anywhere.

---

## What we store

The extension keeps a small amount of data **on your device only**, using the browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). This may include:

- **Your preferences** — such as the selected theme and any display or accessibility settings you choose.
- **In-app state** — such as the last emotion or wheel position you viewed, so the extension can restore where you left off.
- **Optional usage state** — such as recently selected emotions or simple local counts, used only to power features inside the extension (for example, remembering what you looked at last).

This data lives in your browser, stays under your control, and is removed if you uninstall the extension or clear its storage. We never see it, and it never leaves your device.

## What we transmit

**Zero data is transmitted externally.** Specifically:

- **No analytics.** We do not track how you use the extension.
- **No telemetry.** We collect no usage statistics, diagnostics, or identifiers.
- **No servers.** The extension talks to no backend — there is nothing to send data to.
- **No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud sync.**

Everything the extension does happens locally in your browser.

## Permissions

The extension declares exactly one permission:

- **`storage`** — Used solely to save your preferences and in-app state (theme, settings, last-viewed wheel state) on your device so they persist between sessions. It is **not** used to collect, share, or transmit any information. This is local storage only; it does not grant access to anything outside the extension's own saved data.

We request no permissions beyond what this single feature set requires.

## Host permissions

**None.** The extension requests **no host permissions** and **no access to any website.** It does not read, modify, or monitor the pages you visit.

Because it has no host access, the extension **cannot and does not**:

- collect your browsing history or the URLs you visit,
- read or alter the content of any web page,
- inject ads, trackers, or any remote code into pages.

The emotion wheel runs entirely within its own extension UI.

## Third-party services

**None.** The extension uses no third-party services, SDKs, libraries-as-a-service, fonts loaded from external servers, or analytics providers. There are no third parties involved in any way.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects **no personal data from anyone**, including children under the age of 13. Because nothing is collected, stored remotely, or transmitted, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly — or unknowingly — gather personal information from children, because we gather none at all.
