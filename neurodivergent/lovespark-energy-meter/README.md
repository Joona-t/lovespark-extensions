# LoveSpark Energy Meter

Track your daily energy levels (1-5) with one tap. A private, offline energy tracker and mood log built neurodivergent-first.

Part of the [LoveSpark Suite](https://github.com/Joona-t) — retro pink productivity tools for a calmer internet.

## Why

If your energy comes in waves — big mornings and flat afternoons, or the reverse — a quick, low-effort log helps you notice the pattern instead of guessing. Logging takes one tap, the scale is only five points, and there is nothing to configure before you start.

Who it helps:
- People with ADHD, autism, or other neurodivergence who experience uneven or unpredictable energy
- Anyone managing fatigue, burnout recovery, or limited "spoons" who wants a lightweight daily energy log
- Focus-minded folks who want to match hard tasks to their natural energy peaks

## Features

- One-tap energy logging on a simple 1-5 scale
- Today's energy curve, so you can see your ups and downs at a glance
- A pattern-friendly view that helps you spot recurring peaks and dips over the day
- No account, no sign-up, no setup — open the popup and log
- Works fully offline
- OpenDyslexic font option, calm retro-pink theme, high-contrast colors, full keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support

## Privacy

Your energy logs **never leave your device**. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no tracking. Everything is stored locally via `chrome.storage.local`. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for full details.
