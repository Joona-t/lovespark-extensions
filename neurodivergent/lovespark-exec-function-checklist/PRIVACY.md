# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Executive Function Checklist

**Effective date:** June 9, 2026
**Version:** 1.0.0

LoveSpark Executive Function Checklist ("the extension") is a free, open-source browser extension that helps you walk through an ADHD-friendly pre-task checklist — what you need, what step one is, how long it will take, and what could distract you. Your privacy is built into the design: **everything happens locally on your own device.**

---

## What We Store

The extension stores data **only** in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`), on your own computer. This may include:

- **Your checklist content** — the prompts, steps, and notes you write while walking through a task (e.g. what you need, your first step, time estimates, distractions to manage).
- **Your settings and preferences** — such as theme choice, accessibility options, and any toggles you set.
- **Usage counters and progress** — lightweight local values like the number of checklists completed or any streak/progress indicators, if you have those features enabled.

This data lives on your device only. It is never uploaded, synced to us, or shared with anyone. You can clear it at any time by removing the extension or clearing its storage in your browser.

---

## What We Transmit

**Zero.** The extension transmits **no data** to any external server, ever.

- No analytics.
- No telemetry.
- No tracking.
- No crash reporting.
- No remote servers of any kind.

Nothing you type or do in the extension leaves your device.

---

## Permissions

The extension declares only the permissions it strictly needs, and uses each one solely for its stated purpose:

- **`storage`** — Used to save your checklists, settings, and progress locally in your browser via `chrome.storage.local`, so your work persists between sessions. This permission only reads and writes data on your own device; it never sends data anywhere.

---

## Host Permissions

**None.** The extension requests **no host permissions** and **no access to any websites**.

Because of this, the extension:

- Does **not** read, monitor, or collect your browsing history.
- Does **not** access the content of any web page you visit.
- Does **not** inject ads, trackers, or any scripts into web pages.

It simply runs as a self-contained tool you open when you need it.

---

## Third-Party Services

**None.** The extension uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics providers, advertising networks, or external APIs. There are no remote code dependencies and no outside parties involved in any way.

---

## Children's Privacy

The extension collects **no personal data from anyone**, including children under the age of 13. Because all data stays on the user's own device and nothing is ever transmitted, the extension is safe for users of all ages.

---

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published in the public source repository alongside the extension.
