# LoveSpark Gratitude Journal

Daily gratitude journal for ADHD brains: jot 3 things you're grateful for, grow a garden, build a mindful habit. Free & private.

Part of the [LoveSpark Suite](https://github.com/Joona-t) — retro pink productivity tools for a calmer internet.

## Why

Long-form journaling apps ask for more attention than a scattered day can spare, so they get abandoned by day three. This extension keeps the ask small (three short lines), keeps the reward visible (a little garden that grows as you keep showing up), and keeps the whole thing one click away. Micro-gratitude that's actually doable is gratitude that actually sticks.

## Features

- Write 3 things you're grateful for each day — quick, low-friction micro-gratitude
- A garden that grows as your daily-gratitude habit builds consistency
- Designed for ADHD and neurodivergent focus: small ask, visible reward, one click away
- OpenDyslexic font, retro-pink theme, high-contrast, keyboard-navigable, reduced-motion friendly
- 100% local storage — your entries never leave your device
- No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking

## Privacy

Your journal entries **never leave your device**. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no tracking. Everything is stored locally via `chrome.storage.local`. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for full details.
