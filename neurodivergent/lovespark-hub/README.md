# LoveSpark Hub

Your ADHD command center in every new tab: daily planner, focus mode, and gentle break reminders. Free, open source, zero tracking.

Part of the [LoveSpark Suite](https://github.com/Joona-t) — retro pink productivity tools for a calmer internet.

## Features

- Daily planner — jot down today's tasks and check them off on every new tab
- Focus mode — commit to one thing and quiet the noise while you work
- Break reminders — gentle, alarm-scheduled nudges to step away and reset
- Sparky — the LoveSpark mascot, cheering you on instead of guilt-tripping you
- OpenDyslexic-friendly typography, retro-pink theme, full keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support
- Zero data collection — everything stays on your device

## Privacy

Your plan, tasks, and settings **never leave your device**. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no tracking. All data is stored locally in `chrome.storage.local`. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for the full policy.
