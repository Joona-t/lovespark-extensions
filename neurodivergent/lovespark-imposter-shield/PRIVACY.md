# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Imposter Shield

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Imposter Shield is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

The accomplishments you add to your evidence vault (up to 100 entries, each with its text, the date added and an internal id), daily and total "shields" counters (with the date of the last daily reset), the on/off setting, and your theme preference. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Imposter Shield makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. Fonts and styles are packaged with the extension. The popup footer has links to lovespark.love and ko-fi.com that open in a new tab only if you click them. No data ever leaves your device.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your evidence vault, counters, on/off setting and theme locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
