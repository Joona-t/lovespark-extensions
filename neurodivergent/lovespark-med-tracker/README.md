# LoveSpark Med Tracker

Privacy-first medication tracking with one-click dose logging. Never wonder "did I take my meds?" again.

Part of the [LoveSpark Suite](https://github.com/Joona-t) — retro pink productivity tools for a calmer internet.

## Features

- One-click dose logging from your browser toolbar
- Visual progress ring and streak tracking
- Calendar history with adherence dots
- Pharmacokinetic timeline for stimulant medications
- Supply counter with low-stock warnings
- Optional gentle reminders via browser notifications
- Printable doctor report export
- Dark Rose mode
- Zero data collection — everything stays on your device

## Privacy

Your medication data **never leaves your device**. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no tracking. All data is stored locally in `chrome.storage.local`.
