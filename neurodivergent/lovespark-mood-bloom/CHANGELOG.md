# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-03-22

### Added
- Emoji-based mood picker (Amazing, Good, Okay, Low, Rough)
- Optional quick notes with each check-in (140 char max)
- Weekly garden visualization showing mood flowers
- Streak tracking with growing plant indicators
- Daily and total check-in counters
- 4 themes: Retro Pink, Dark Noir, Warm Beige, Cool Slate
- Full WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility (keyboard nav, ARIA, screen reader support)
- Privacy-first: all data stored locally, no tracking, no accounts
