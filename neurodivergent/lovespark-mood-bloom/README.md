# LoveSpark Mood Bloom

Mood tracker that grows a flower garden. ADHD-friendly emoji check-ins with daily reminders. No account, no tracking, always free.

Part of the [LoveSpark Suite](https://github.com/Joona-t) — retro pink productivity tools for a calmer internet.

## Features

- Quick daily mood check-ins — log how you feel in a couple of taps
- ADHD-friendly emoji picker — big, clear, low-friction, no typing required
- A garden that blooms over time — your logged moods grow into flowers
- Gentle, optional daily reminders via browser alarms
- See your patterns — a visible history of your moods, right in your browser
- OpenDyslexic-friendly typography, retro-pink theme, full keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support
- Zero data collection — everything stays on your device

## Privacy

Your mood check-ins and garden state **never leave your device**. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics, no tracking. All data is stored locally in `chrome.storage.local`. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for the full policy.
