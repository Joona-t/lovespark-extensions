# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Noise Level

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Noise Level is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

The 1–5 noise ratings you log, kept as your most recent rating, a per-weekday running total (sum and count) used for the weekly pattern chart, a count of logs today and in total, and the date of the last daily reset. Your theme choice is also saved. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. The extension does not use microphone access and does not measure sound; ratings are only what you click. You can remove all of it at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Noise Level makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. No data ever leaves your device.

The popup footer contains two ordinary links (lovespark.love and a Ko-fi page). They open in a new tab only if you click them, and no data is sent with them.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your noise ratings, weekly pattern, log counts and theme choice locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
