# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Reward Spinner

**Effective date:** 2026-07-08 · **Applies to:** LoveSpark Reward Spinner v1.0.31

LoveSpark Reward Spinner is a free, open-source browser extension that lets you spin a wheel of self-chosen rewards after completing a task. Your privacy is not a feature we bolt on — it is the architecture. This extension collects **no** personal data, sends **nothing** to any server, and works entirely on your own device.

---

## What we store

Everything the extension saves lives **only** on your own computer, inside your browser's `chrome.storage.local`. Nothing is stored anywhere else. Specifically, this may include:

- **Your reward list** — the custom rewards you add to the wheel.
- **Extension settings and preferences** — such as your chosen theme and any display options.
- **Local activity data** — for example, spin counts, completion counters, or streaks used to power the gamified experience.

This data exists so the extension can remember your rewards and preferences between sessions. It never leaves your device. You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the extension's storage or by removing the extension from your browser.

---

## What we transmit

**Zero.** No data is transmitted externally — ever.

- **No** analytics.
- **No** telemetry or usage tracking.
- **No** external servers, APIs, or cloud sync.
- **No** accounts, logins, or profiles.

The extension has no network functionality for sending your data anywhere. It runs fully offline, on your machine, using only local browser storage.

---

## Permissions

The extension requests the minimum permissions required for its single purpose, and nothing more:

- **`storage`** — Used solely to save your reward list, settings, and local counters/streaks on your own device via `chrome.storage.local`, so they persist between sessions. This permission does **not** grant any network access and is **not** used to transmit, share, or sync your data.

No other permissions are requested.

---

## Host permissions

**None.** This extension requests no host permissions and no access to any websites.

Because it holds no host permissions, it **cannot** and **does not**:

- Read, track, or collect your browsing history.
- Access the content of any web page you visit.
- Inject ads, trackers, scripts, or any other content into web pages.
- Monitor your activity across sites.

The extension operates entirely within its own popup interface and local storage.

---

## Third-party services

**None.** The extension uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics providers, advertising networks, or external code of any kind. There is no one to share data with, because no data ever leaves your device.

---

## Children's privacy

This extension collects **no** personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because it stores nothing beyond your own local rewards and settings and transmits nothing externally, it is safe for users of all ages. It complies with children's privacy protections by design — there is simply no data collection to protect against.
