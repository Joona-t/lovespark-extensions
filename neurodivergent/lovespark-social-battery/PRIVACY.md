# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Social Battery

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Social Battery is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

Your social-battery check-ins and display preferences (such as theme). Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Social Battery makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. No data ever leaves your device.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your own content and settings locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
