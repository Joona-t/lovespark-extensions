# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Stim Board

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Stim Board is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

Only simple counters and preferences: how many stims you did today and in total, the date of the last daily reset, the clicker count, whether the extension is enabled, and your chosen theme. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Stim Board makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. All styles and scripts are packaged inside the extension; no external fonts or CDNs are loaded. No data ever leaves your device.

The popup footer contains two ordinary links (the LoveSpark website and a Ko-fi page). They open in a new tab only if you click them, and send no data from the extension.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your stim counters, clicker count, on/off setting and theme locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
