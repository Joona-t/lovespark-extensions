# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Task Starter

**Effective date:** June 9, 2026
**Version:** 1.0.0

LoveSpark Task Starter is a free, open-source browser extension that helps you break task paralysis in three small steps. Your privacy is built into how it works: everything stays on your own device. This policy explains exactly what that means.

## What we store

LoveSpark Task Starter saves a small amount of data **locally on your device only**, using the browser's `chrome.storage.local` API. Depending on how you use the extension, this may include:

- **Your current task and its steps** — the task you name, the tiny first step you identify, and your progress through the 3-step flow.
- **Your settings and preferences** — for example, your chosen theme and any options you toggle in the extension.
- **Optional progress counters / streaks** — local tallies such as how many tasks you've started or completed, used only to display your own progress back to you.

This data lives in your browser. It is never sent anywhere, never shared, and you can clear it at any time by removing the extension or clearing its storage.

## What we transmit

**ZERO data is transmitted externally.**

- No analytics.
- No telemetry.
- No tracking.
- No remote servers — there is no backend to talk to.
- No accounts and no sign-in.

The extension functions entirely offline, on your device. Nothing about you or your tasks ever leaves your browser.

## Permissions

LoveSpark Task Starter requests the minimum permissions required for its single purpose.

- **`storage`** — Used to save your task, steps, settings, and optional progress counters locally in `chrome.storage.local` so they persist between sessions. This permission is used **only** for local on-device storage. It does not enable any network access and is never used to transmit, sync, or share your data.

## Host permissions

**None.**

LoveSpark Task Starter requests **no host permissions** and **no access to any websites**. It does not read, monitor, or modify the pages you visit. Specifically, it does **not**:

- collect your browsing history,
- read or alter web page content,
- inject ads, trackers, or scripts into any site,
- access your tabs, cookies, or network requests.

## Third-party services

**None.**

The extension uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics providers, advertising networks, or external code of any kind. It serves no ads and runs no remote code.

## Children's privacy

LoveSpark Task Starter collects **no personal data from anyone**, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is ever collected or transmitted, the extension is safe for users of all ages.
