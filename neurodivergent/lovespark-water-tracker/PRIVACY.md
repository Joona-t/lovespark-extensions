# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Water Tracker

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Water Tracker is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

Your water counts (glasses logged today, your all-time total, your daily goal), the date of the last daily reset, whether tracking is switched on, and your theme preference. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Water Tracker makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. Fonts are bundled inside the extension, not loaded from a font service. The popup footer contains links to lovespark.love and Ko-fi that open only if you click them. No data ever leaves your device.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your water counts, daily goal and settings locally so they are still there next time. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
