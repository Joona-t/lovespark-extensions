# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Working Memory Pad

**Effective date:** June 9, 2026 · **Version:** 1.0.0

LoveSpark Working Memory Pad is a free, open-source browser extension that helps you hold onto things you'd otherwise forget by pinning up to 3 quick notes that stay visible when you open the popup. Your privacy is not a feature we added — it's the foundation. This extension keeps everything on your own device and sends nothing, to anyone, ever.

## What we store

The extension stores data **only** on your own computer, using the browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). Specifically, this is limited to:

- **Your pinned notes** — the text you type into your working memory pad (up to 3 notes), such as phone numbers, instructions, or reminders.
- **Your preferences** — small settings like your chosen theme, so the popup looks the way you left it.

That's it. This data lives on your device, is readable only by the extension, and is removed if you uninstall the extension or clear its storage.

## What we transmit

**Zero data is transmitted externally. None. Ever.**

- No analytics.
- No telemetry.
- No tracking or usage reporting.
- No accounts, sign-in, or sync servers.
- No external servers of any kind.

Nothing you type or configure ever leaves your device. There is no network code in this extension that sends your data anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go — everything stays local.

## Permissions

The extension requests the minimum permissions required for its single purpose. Each one is explained below.

- **`storage`** — Used to save your pinned notes and preferences locally on your device, so they're still there the next time you open the popup. This permission only enables local storage; it does **not** grant any network or remote-data access. Your notes are stored on your machine and nowhere else.

## Host permissions

**This extension requests no host permissions.** It does not request access to any websites, tabs, or page content.

Because there are no host permissions:

- It does **not** read, monitor, or collect your browsing history.
- It does **not** read the content of any web pages you visit.
- It does **not** inject ads, trackers, scripts, or any other content into the pages you browse.

The extension's notepad lives entirely inside its own popup.

## Third-party services

**None.** The extension uses no third-party services, SDKs, libraries-as-a-service, advertising networks, or analytics providers. There are no outside parties involved in any way.

## Children's privacy

This extension collects **no personal data from anyone**, including children under the age of 13. Because nothing is collected, stored remotely, or transmitted, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly — or unknowingly — gather information about any user, child or adult.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be published in the public source repository, and the **Effective date** and **Version** at the top of this document will be revised. Because the extension is open source, the full history of any change is visible in the repository's commit log.
