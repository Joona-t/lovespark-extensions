💕 About

LoveSpark Twitch AdBlock strips ad segments from Twitch's HLS stream before the player sees them. If any ads slip through, they're automatically muted with a pretty overlay until the stream resumes.

Two layers of protection: stream-level ad stripping + DOM-based mute fallback. Part of the LoveSpark Suite.
