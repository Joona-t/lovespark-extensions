# Privacy Policy — LoveSpark Daily Checklist

_Last updated: 2026-10-08_

LoveSpark Daily Checklist is a free, open-source LoveSpark browser extension. It is built to be private by design.

## What is stored

Today's checklist items (the task text you type and whether each is done), the date of the current list (used to clear the list each new day), counters of tasks completed today and in total, an on/off flag, and your theme preference. Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. The checklist items are cleared automatically each new day; you can remove everything at any time by uninstalling the extension.

## What is transmitted

**Nothing.** LoveSpark Daily Checklist makes no network requests of its own. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, no accounts, and no remote code. No data ever leaves your device.

The popup footer contains two ordinary links (lovespark.love and ko-fi.com) that open in a new tab only if you click them; no data is attached to them.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | Saves your checklist items, completion counters, on/off state and theme locally so they are still there next time you open the popup. |

The extension requests **no host permissions** and runs **no content scripts**: it cannot read or change the web pages you visit.

## Third-party services

None.

## Children's privacy

The extension collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
