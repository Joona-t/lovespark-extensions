How it works & FAQ

How it works

  • Every new tab opens your notes. Tap + (or ⌘/Ctrl+N) to start one; it saves as you type.
  • Sort notes with the category tabs. + New category makes your own, with a name, emoji and colour.
  • Pick a category for a note from the menu at the top of the editor.
  • • List turns lines into bullet points. Typing - and a space at the start of a line does it too, Enter continues the list, and Enter on an empty bullet ends it.
  • On a bullet line, Tab and Shift+Tab indent and outdent. Press Esc first and Tab moves on to the next control as usual.
  • Tags, pinning, search (⌘/Ctrl+K), list or grid view, and six themes are all in the top bar.

FAQ

Where are my notes stored?

Only in this browser, on this device. There's no account, no cloud, no tracking, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I lose my notes?

Removing the extension or clearing this browser's extension data deletes them, so copy anything important somewhere safe first.

How do I make my own category?

Click + New category, give it a name and pick a colour. To rename or delete one, open its tab and click Edit category. Deleting a category never deletes notes: they move to Uncategorized.

How do bullet points work?

They're plain text, a • at the start of a line, so they stay readable anywhere you paste them. Select several lines and press • List to bullet or un-bullet them all at once.

Is it really free?

Yes, always. No ads, no data collection, and it's open source under the MIT licence.
